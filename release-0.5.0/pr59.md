## ciris-kernel 0.5.0

### What 0.5.0 is

The kernel now decides who holds what over a room, and how a row counts, from rows alone. No stated instant
decides a verdict, and the host clock decides none.

- **The seat model.** Every seat is two consents. A seat ends by ancestry, never by an instant. A counted act
  names the newest counted changes in its own past (heads H1 to H8). Rule 5 holds on every counted act. One seat
  walk reads families, infrastructure communities (M of N), affiliations with officers, and the lone duty acts
  (quarantine, slashing, takedown, reconsideration).
- **Persons.** Every count is a count of persons. A person is named by its self roster's genesis key; its other
  live keys are its devices (`seat::Persons`). Officers are counted by person too. One self roster per identity is
  read by the reader's line.
- **The key view.** Every count's voices step reads the key view first. A dead key is out, and only then are the
  live keys mapped to persons (`CutSpec::key_view`, `key_end::counts_by`).
- **Windows from the policy table.** A window's rule comes from the pinned governance policy table
  (`window::spec_from_policy`).
- **The consent peer set.** A `consent:replication:v1` grant moves the classes it names to one peer, and nothing
  else (`consent::in_force`, `peer_set`, `may_send`, `replication_set`).
- **The election close.** An election closes on rows (`seat::election_closed`).
- **The wire map and 70 vectors.** `WIRE_MAP.md` maps every row-view field to its `ceg-row/2` source.
  `VECTORS.md` states 70 neutral cases for Verify to mint.

This is a breaking release for adopters. The CHANGELOG's "Adopting 0.5.0 from 0.2.2" lists every change: the new
traits and required methods, the enum and struct changes, what was removed, and the presentation rules that change
answers with no error.

### Stated residuals

Each residual is a case. No rule can close them, because the rows do not show them.

- **One or two keys.** A self roster's evicted key is no voice of its own eviction. So a roster whose only live key
  is its genesis key cannot evict it. A two-key roster evicts only by the lone path, when the other key is silent
  through the window. A one-key person whose key is stolen and then revoked has no other device to withdraw with.
- **The lone-add entrenchment** (CC 3.3.6.4, in the Constitution's words): "a holder of one key of three that adds
  a key while one other is silent holds half the roster, and no count or lone path removes it". It goes to the
  steward after the tag.
- **Unlinked keys.** Two keys one person keeps unlinked, in no shared roster, are two persons to every rule.
  Persons are read in the act's own past.
- **A fresh reader holding rivals waits.** A reader whose line takes up neither rival founding, or both, reads no
  person through them. Adopter duty: a client holding a genesis key and no roster rows fetches the identity's
  roster before it signs anything, and never founds where one may exist.
- **The FD1 split** (r103). The signer's own concurrent regrants, each naming one fork, settle nothing; the fold
  fails closed. The next regrant naming both settles it.
